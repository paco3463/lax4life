Age groups

Players train with kids their own age and level. Each group has its own coaching focus, from first ground balls to college-style systems.

U10–U11

Youth

First sticks and first goals. Fun, fast games that build skills without the pressure.

  • Catching, throwing, cradling
  • Ground balls and footwork
  • Small-sided 4v4 and 7v7
U12–U13

Junior

Two-handed players who understand the field. The jump from rec to club lacrosse.

  • Off-hand development
  • Dodging and shooting on the run
  • Intro to team offense and defense
U14–U15

Select

Full-field lacrosse and tournament play, built to get players ready for high school varsity.

  • Position-specific training
  • Clearing, rides and transition
  • Regional tournaments
U16–U17

Elite

High-level competition with college exposure for players who want to play at the next level.

  • Advanced sets and film review
  • Showcase tournaments
  • Recruiting guidance

How sign-up works

Registration takes about five minutes. You'll get a confirmation email with your schedule and next steps.

  1. Choose a season

    Spring, summer or winter. Each one has its own dates and programs.

  2. Pick a program

    League, academy, camp or travel team. Your player's age group is matched from their birthday.

  3. Fill out the form

    Player info, a parent contact, jersey size and any medical notes for coaches.

  4. Get on the field

    We confirm your spot by email with payment details, field location and what to bring.

Draw control, stick skills and girls' rules game play, taught by former high school and college players.

What to bring

New to the sport? Loaner sticks are available for first-season players. Mark it on the registration form.

Boys

  • NOCSAE-certified helmet
  • Gloves
  • Shoulder pads
  • Arm pads
  • Mouthguard
  • Athletic cup
  • Stick (40–42")
  • Cleats
  • Water bottle
  • Goalies: chest and throat guard

Girls

  • Certified eye goggles
  • Mouthguard
  • Girls' stick
  • Cleats
  • Water bottle
  • Optional: soft headgear
  • Goalies: full goalie kit

Gear available locally at Dick's Sporting Goods, or online at sportstop.com and Lacrosse Unlimited.

Questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a message below and a coach will get back to you within a day.

Does my child need lacrosse experience?
No. Every program groups players by age and level, and our Youth and Junior groups are built for first-time players. Loaner sticks are available.
How are age groups decided?
By the player's age on August 31, 2026 for all 2026–27 seasons. A player who is 11 on that date plays U12. Use the age finder above to check.
Do you have girls programs?
Yes. Girls academy and girls teams run in every season, coached under girls' rules with girls' equipment.
What happens when it rains or there's lightning?
Practices move or pause for lightning. We clear the field at the first strike and wait 30 minutes after the last one. Cancellations go out by text and email at least an hour before start time.
What is the refund policy?
Full refund up to 7 days before the season starts. After that we credit missed sessions toward a future season. Registration and cancellation for Pinecrest programs are handled by Pinecrest Parks & Recreation at 305-284-0900.
Do you offer sibling or financial assistance?
Siblings get 10% off each additional player. If cost is a barrier, email us. We don't want any kid to miss out.
